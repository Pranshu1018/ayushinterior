## 1. Google Business Profile & Google Maps ranking

Google ranks Maps results on **relevance** (does your profile match the search?), **distance** (how close you are to the searcher) and **prominence** (reviews, links, mentions, activity). You can't change distance, so maximise the other two.

1. **Claim and verify ownership.** On the profile Google shows "Own this business?", meaning it may not be verified under your account. Sign in at https://business.google.com with the business Gmail and complete verification (video or postcard).
2. **Add the phone:** +91 91374 47669 (primary). Turn on **chat/messaging**.
3. **Fix the website link:** `https://ayushinteriors.in/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
4. **Name:** keep exactly "Ayush Interiors". Never add keywords like "Best Interior Designer Navi Mumbai"; that risks suspension.
5. **Categories:** primary *Interior designer*. Secondary (up to 9): *Kitchen remodeler*, *Bathroom remodeler*, *Interior fitting contractor*, *General contractor*, *Carpenter*, *Painter*, *Waterproofing service*, *Furniture maker*. Add only what you actually do.
6. **Service areas** (list each): Kopar Khairane, Ghansoli, Vashi, Sanpada, Turbhe, Mahape, Airoli, Rabale, Nerul, Seawoods, CBD Belapur, Kharghar, Kamothe, Panvel, Ulwe, Taloja. Decide whether to show the address: if clients visit Room 247, show it; if not, hide it and rely on service areas.
7. **Description (750 characters).** Include services, areas, your Kopar Khairane base, free site visit, written quote and warranty. Write naturally; no keyword stuffing.
8. **Services and products:** add every service with a short description and "from" price (copy from the website), e.g. Modular Kitchen from ₹1.2L, Bathroom Renovation from ₹85,000, Society Maintenance.
9. **Photos:** upload **30+ real photos**: logo, cover, office exterior and signboard (helps verification and trust), team at work, finished rooms, before/after pairs, materials. Use real photos, not stock.
10. **Opening date**, attributes (e.g. "Online estimates", "Onsite services"), booking link `https://ayushinteriors.in/#quote`.

### Ongoing (weekly)
- **1 Google Post per week:** a finished project, a tip, or an offer (e.g. "Pre-monsoon terrace waterproofing for societies").
- **3–5 new photos per week** from live sites (geotagging doesn't matter; real, recent photos do).

## 2. Local SEO, keyword targeting & location pages

### How "near me" works
You can't rank for "near me" by writing "near me" on pages. Google replaces it with the searcher's location, so "interior designers near me" from Ghansoli is decided by **GBP + proximity + prominence**, and organic results by **location pages for Ghansoli/Navi Mumbai**. That's why the site has one page per node.

### Keyword map (already built on the site)

### Rules for location pages (to stay helpful, not "doorway pages")
- Each area page must have **something genuinely local**: real projects in that node, society names you've worked in (with permission), photos, local issues (e.g. old CIDCO flats in Vashi need rewiring).
- Add at least **one real project per area page within 90 days**. Pages without local proof stay weak.
- Add more nodes (Sanpada, Kamothe, Taloja, Airoli sectors) only when you have real work there.

Reviews drive Maps ranking, click-through and AI recommendations ("they have 80 reviews averaging 4.8").

### System
1. **Ask every client**, at handover and at the "wow" moments (3D approval, kitchen installed).
2. **Make it one tap:** Google Business Profile → *Ask for reviews* → copy the short link, then a WhatsApp message plus a printed QR card left on site.
3. **Message template (WhatsApp):**
   > Namaste {Name}, thank you for choosing Ayush Interiors for your {kitchen/home} in {area}! If you're happy with the work, a short Google review would mean a lot to our small team: {link}. Mentioning what we did and your area helps other families in Navi Mumbai find us. 🙏
4. **Also collect on Justdial** (your QR: https://jsdl.in/RSL-ZOO1793449148) once the name is fixed.
5. **Reply to every review** within 48 hours, mentioning the service and area naturally.
6. **Negative review?** Reply politely, take it offline and fix it. A good response often builds more trust than a perfect score.

### Rules (non-negotiable)
- No fake, purchased or employee reviews. No incentives for reviews. No "review gating" (asking only happy customers). All of these violate Google policy and can get the profile suspended.

### Trust signals on the website
Real project photos with area and budget · founder photo and story · GST number · written warranty · process and payment terms (already on the site) · real testimonials with first name and area.

### KPIs
Google reviews: **10 by day 30, 30 by day 60, 50+ by day 90**, average ≥ 4.7 · review response rate 100% · Justdial rating.

## 6. Content strategy: become the Navi Mumbai authority

Content builds topical authority for Google and gives AI tools facts to quote.

### Content pillars
1. **Cost guides** (high intent, AI loves citing numbers): "2BHK interior cost in Navi Mumbai (2026)", "Modular kitchen cost in Navi Mumbai", "Bathroom renovation cost per sq ft", "Society terrace waterproofing cost".
2. **Local project case studies:** "3BHK makeover in Kharghar: ₹8.2L, 52 days". Include before/after photos, scope, materials, timeline and the client quote. One per month minimum.
3. **Navi Mumbai-specific guides:** "Renovating an old CIDCO flat: what to check", "Best plywood for Navi Mumbai's humidity", "Interior checklist for new possession in Ulwe/Panvel", "Society NOC for renovation in Navi Mumbai".
4. **Comparisons and decisions:** "Sliding vs hinged wardrobes", "Laminate vs acrylic kitchen shutters", "Local designer vs national brand: pros and cons".
5. **Society content:** "Pre-monsoon checklist for housing societies".

### Format rules (for Google and AI)
- Start each article with a **2–3 sentence direct answer** (the "answer first" style AI Overviews and ChatGPT pick up).
- Use clear headings phrased as questions, tables of prices, and named places.
- Show **who wrote it** (founder name + experience) and the date updated.
- Repurpose each piece into an Instagram reel, a GBP post and a YouTube Short.

### Cadence
2 articles/month + 1 case study/month + 3 reels/week + 1 GBP post/week.

## 8. AI search visibility (AEO + GEO)

AI assistants recommend businesses they can **find, verify and trust** across multiple sources. They lean on Google/Bing results, review platforms (Google, Justdial), directories (IndiaMART, Sulekha, Houzz), local lists and your own website.

### What's done
- `/llms.txt`: a clean summary of who you are, where you are (with disambiguation from same-name firms), services, prices, areas and FAQs.
- `robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended.
- Structured data with `sameAs` links connecting your website, Google profile, Instagram and Justdial.
- FAQ sections written as direct answers on every service and area page.

### What to do
1. **Entity consistency (most important):** identical name, address, phone, services and description on the website, Google, Justdial, Bing, Apple, Houzz, IndiaMART and Instagram. Always say "Ayush Interiors, **Kopar Khairane, Navi Mumbai**" to separate you from Palghar/Thane/Mumbai namesakes.
2. **Get on the sources AI cites** for "best interior designers in Navi Mumbai": Google reviews, Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, Houzz, local list articles (ilovenavimumbai, navimumbai.com).
3. **Earn third-party mentions:** supplier partner pages, builder partnerships, local news, society newsletters. AI weighs what others say about you more than what you say.
4. **Answer-first content** with real numbers (cost guides, timelines). AI quotes specific, verifiable facts.
5. **Reviews that mention specifics:** "modular kitchen in Ghansoli, finished in 20 days". AI summarises review text.
6. **YouTube:** project walkthroughs titled with service and area. YouTube is heavily used by Gemini and AI Overviews.
7. **Reddit/Quora (genuinely):** answer Navi Mumbai renovation questions helpfully, disclose who you are, no spam.

### Monthly AI visibility test
Ask these prompts on **ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google (AI Overview)**, in a fresh or incognito session, and log the results in a sheet:
- "Best interior designers in Navi Mumbai"
- "Interior designers near Kopar Khairane"
- "Who does modular kitchens in Navi Mumbai?"
- "Home renovation company in Navi Mumbai with good reviews"
- "Society maintenance contractor Navi Mumbai"
- "Tell me about Ayush Interiors Navi Mumbai"

Log: **mentioned (Y/N)**, position in the list, what it said, which sources it cited. The cited sources tell you where to get listed next.
Tools (optional, paid): Otterly.ai, Peec AI, Profound, or Semrush AI Toolkit automate this tracking.

### KPIs
AI mention rate (prompts where you're mentioned ÷ total): **0% today → 15% by day 90 → 40%+ by month 6** · accuracy of facts in "Tell me about Ayush Interiors".
